## What this file does NOT establish

- The grades are one person's judgement on one viewing, not a controlled perceptual study, so treat A/B/C as an ordinal label and not a measurement.
- Clips are not independent: several are trimmed segments and the batch over-represents whichever breeds were easiest to source. Proportions describe THIS dataset.
- An effect size computed on tens of clips is an indication of direction, not a significance test.
